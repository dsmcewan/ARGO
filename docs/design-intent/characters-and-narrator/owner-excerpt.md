The idea
A character does not carry what other people feel about them. Each person who dealt with the traveler, or stood nearby, holds their own impression, read through their own nature. Characters are busy with their own lives and say very little. The narrator is a tired, sarcastic onlooker who nobody in the world can reach, and he is the only one who holds the full record of what the traveler said and did.
