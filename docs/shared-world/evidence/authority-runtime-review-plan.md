## Performance evidence, separate from correctness

On the exact candidate, construct signed 128/512/1,000/2,000-event histories with realistic speech/replies plus some impressions, offers, acceptance and fulfillment. Keep the fixture/model-free. Report Node/platform, history/state byte sizes, actor/item counts, samples, median, p95/max and parent event-loop delay separately for cold open, unchanged scoped read/snapshot, speech append, exact retry and second-handle suffix refresh. Separate process/schema startup, history generation and timed work.

Inspect profiles or safe call counts to establish that unchanged warm reads/retries avoid repeated signature verification and full historical validation. Time alone does not prove cache correctness. Whole-state hashing and serialization may remain proportional to state size, and repeated cold-prefix hashing may be quadratic; do not promise linear replay. Compare results with actual host deadlines and declared supported sizes, identifying any shortfall before release rather than inventing an unapproved latency SLA. Also exercise the 16 MiB/structural limit in a separate capacity probe; the million-event ceiling is not a promise that every history fits.
