## Contract intent is coherent

**Encounter scope/recovery (lines 13–19).** A separate mortal-rules/0.3.0 authority, domain and SQLite entry point preserves v2 files and behavior and rejects silent database/genesis conversion. One encounter remains, with its entire roster and site locked, while nonparticipants elsewhere may progress and pay only their own active-time costs. The encounter pauses locally as serialized world time advances. Recovery uses an authenticated configured referee, an encounter identity plus turn-start token, a normal current cursor, and a recorded deterministic pass/close action. A timeout reason is a host attestation rather than proof that elapsed wall time caused gameplay. The conservative escaped-roster lock retains co-location and cost assumptions. None of this claims multiple encounters or simultaneous physical simulation.

**Curated actor knowledge (lines 23–27).** The closed 0.1 supplement and campaign pack stay unchanged. A versioned sidecar selects actor-owned, time/sequence-eligible, source-verified knowledge under a full-scope exact-query host grant. Only the selected records/provenance reach the model; refreshed fingerprints suppress output when authorized context changes. Authored input remains presentation rather than an acquired experience, impression evidence ID, world fact, accepted bargain, custody transfer or consent. QUICKSTART/ENCOUNTERS support free loan, no deposit, traveler-supplied oil, an explicitly agreed return checkpoint, and disclosed actual repair capped at ordinary five-obol replacement value. Include the authored oil price/duration—one obol for six exploration turns—in the curated operational terms. Do not import private_knowledge, variant truth, referee context or sample speech wholesale.

**Restrictive NEW observation writes (lines 31–35).** The new authenticated ingress policy contains source impersonation and unrelated-recipient injection while leaving historical v1 signature/rule replay untouched. Self-only private observations and exact private system character carriers remain admissible. External recipients require a proposal/principal/scope/cursor/recipient-bound host grant; party membership alone is not evidence. Exact historical retries may return the prior receipt without appending or fabricating a new grant. Existing unsafe claims are not erased, relabeled or retroactively certified, and ordinary legacy claims still do not enter CharacterView. Persisted signed witness evidence and migration remain explicit future work.

The design also correctly preserves the unresolved character/mortal identity-and-clock integration boundary, uses disjoint ownership after reviewed freezes, retains old board evidence/statuses, and requires the separately trusted exact-candidate application gate. These three repairs must not be presented as a unified playable game or as remediation of the unseen fourteen findings.
