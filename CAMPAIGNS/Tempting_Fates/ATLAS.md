# Kymene atlas — referee edition

This contains referee truth, private NPC notes and initial custody. Give players [PLAYER_GUIDE.md](PLAYER_GUIDE.md) or the minimal catalog handout, not this book or world.json. These are original campaign additions; [the owner scenario](../../SCENARIOS/Tempted_Fates.md) remains controlling.

## The island and its roads

Kymene occupies a small unnamed island. Ordinary farms and other harbors exist beyond the keyed area; establish them as needed rather than sealing the traveler inside this map. Dark miracles below are facts of this campaign continuity, not assertions that all Greek traditions agree. Local authorities may still lie about them. The gods have specific limits and demands; there is no universal guilt meter.

One turn is 600 seconds (10 minutes); one watch is 24 turns (4 hours). The day is 86,400 seconds and the synthetic year 360 days. Walking times below exclude stops. Announce load/weather adjustments before accepting travel. Routes are undirected. The loom has no route or guaranteed arrival.

## Keyed sites

### Kymene harbor (l_harbor)

**Mythic concern:** Xenia and Poseidon: welcome offered beside a sea that keeps its own accounts.

**Observable:** Empty boats knock at their moorings. Melia trims an ordinary clay lamp beside her kiln. A water basin is open to strangers; two notices offer grain work and crossing repairs.

**Referee truth:** Start at world tick 0. Empty-oar sounds are a real, harmless omen in this continuity, not proof that a particular drowned person speaks. Melia owns traveler_lamp; seeing it or accepting hospitality gives no custody or debt. The two jobs are independent of the relic. The ordinary routes remain open.

**At seed:** Melia. **Routes:** r_harbor_market, r_harbor_causeway

### Demeter’s granary (l_granary)

**Mythic concern:** Demeter: seed, hunger, and a sacred future competing with living mouths.

**Observable:** Households queue beside blue-marked seed doors. In lamplight the seed sacks cast thin root-shaped shadows. Thaleia examines grain while Philon separates a feast requisition from the stock list.

**Referee truth:** The household grain is sound; the shadows are a genuine local sign, not spoiled food. No famine appears merely because travelers refuse to help. The seed taboo is posted: knowingly burn blue-marked planting grain for display and the handful you burn turns to biting black husks, causing 1d4 manual damage to the burner; household stocks are not cursed. Ordinary eating is not this taboo. Philon has an unsigned feast request; three distribution work lots remain after the initial count.

**At seed:** Philon, Thaleia. **Routes:** r_market_granary, r_granary_causeway, r_granary_palace

### Poseidon’s broken crossing (l_causeway)

**Mythic concern:** Poseidon: peril, sworn passage and the difference between reverence and concealed danger.

**Observable:** A loose handrail hangs above black water. Dion has stopped a loaded cart; his ferry waits on the safe bank. Wet footprints appear on the empty landing and end at a painted oath stone.

**Referee truth:** A failed pin explains the structural danger. The wet footprints are a real omen but identify no soul. The optional oath reads: declare the load entrusted to this passage. Dion states the consequence before accepting a sworn manifest: a knowingly false sworn load makes that boat take brine, forcing an immediate return or unloading at the near bank. An unsworn safe passage is available; no one must swear. No drowning or arbitrary sacrifice is guaranteed.

**At seed:** Dion. **Routes:** r_harbor_causeway, r_granary_causeway

### Hermes' market of witnesses (l_market)

**Mythic concern:** Hermes: trade, cleverness and the terrible usefulness of an exact witness.

**Observable:** Weights turn without a breeze beneath a painted traveler’s hat. Ione checks a delivery line while a bronze witness tablet repeats the last public sworn bargain in a thin voice.

**Referee truth:** The tablet is a local supernatural witness, not omniscient truth. Its announced rule: knowingly sign a false statement on it and it repeats that signer and statement in their own voice until they publicly correct that statement; it does not discover unrelated secrets. Using it is optional. Ione has measure_cup only in genuine/counterfeit; in legend her case holds correspondence only. Her sender’s palace seal does not authenticate divine authority.

**At seed:** Ione. **Routes:** r_harbor_market, r_market_granary, r_market_palace, r_necropolis_market

### Palace of the borrowed feast (l_palace)

**Mythic concern:** Dionysus: liberation offered as hospitality, corrupted when a host makes enjoyment compulsory.

**Observable:** Grape-painted plaster peels above scrubbed benches. Xanthe lifts a bronze mask; its shadow keeps smiling when she sets it down. The eastern roof rope hangs slack.

**Referee truth:** The mask’s wrong shadow is real but grants no command. The feast’s posted taboo is explicit: after accepting the role of sacred host, knowingly bar a guest’s departure and the host’s own chair roots to the floor with vine, restraining the host until the exit is opened. Guests owe no intoxication, oath, intimacy or life gift. The eastern aisle must be unloaded or closed. The mural records a vessel leaving the quarry, not its present location or power.

**At seed:** Xanthe. **Routes:** r_market_palace, r_palace_quarry, r_granary_palace

### The statue and bronze works (l_quarry)

**Mythic concern:** Hephaestus: craft, dangerous pride and care for hands that gods do not replace.

**Observable:** An empty recess gapes below a headless statue. Makar braces a bronze lifting arm; when the furnace cools, hammering continues once from inside the stone.

**Referee truth:** The statue recess is empty in every variant. The single answering hammerstroke is a real omen, not an instruction or hidden worker. The lifting arm has a visibly worn pawl. Hephaestus’s locally stated workshop vow is optional: someone who swears the brace safe while knowingly releasing it under another worker’s load has the release handle sear their hand for 1d4 manual damage; this adds no shame or disability score. Do not force a vow to buy a repair.

**At seed:** Makar. **Routes:** r_palace_quarry, r_quarry_necropolis

### The road of uncounted names (l_nekropolis)

**Mythic concern:** Burial, Hades and Persephone: duties to the dead without claiming to know their judgment.

**Observable:** Names hang from a cord above the blocked gate. Leon clears a white-stone path. At dusk the stones softly repeat only names families chose to speak there.

**Referee truth:** The unburied fellowship comprises living families and burial workers, not seeded undead. The echo reveals no undisclosed names or afterlife verdict. The painted gate’s prohibition warns: knowingly erase another family’s memorial name to sell the place and the offender’s own newly written public name washes away until the inscription is restored or the family accepts a repair. Memory and identity are not erased. Refusing burial work invokes nothing.

**At seed:** Leon. **Routes:** r_quarry_necropolis, r_necropolis_market

### The loom, if encountered (l_loom)

**Mythic concern:** Three jurisdictions: beginnings, measure and endings; none is a court of virtue.

**Observable:** Wood sounds before it can be seen. Three women attend different tasks. None announces that a traveler has been expected.

**Referee truth:** Off-map and optional. No ordinary route, die roll, purchase or ritual guarantees access. A separately established encounter must include a way to leave. Clotho does not grant years; Lachesis does not choose donors; Atropos does not judge deservingness. No shared omniscient view, forced gift, production anomaly trigger or explanatory epilogue is supplied.

**At seed:** Clotho, Lachesis, Atropos. **Routes:** None; establish an optional encounter separately.

## People whose lives do not orbit the traveler

Starting positions are not a schedule that teleports NPCs to any scene where they would be useful. Their private notes are referee resources, not authenticated actor observations. Share only through legitimate perception or disclosure. Each observer owns their impressions; the subject does not receive a global reputation map. The Fates remain separate principals.

The optional runtime profiles allow 240 Unicode code points per reply and a 60-world-second minimum interval. Dion, Philon and Makar are initially busy through tick 600; others use 0. These are campaign settings, not a literary requirement to speak or an instruction to advance time. A human referee decides whether to interrupt an activity. Age/remaining-year seed values are synthetic initialization, not player prophecies or divine biology.

### Melia — Potter and harbor host (melia)

**Where / visible:** Kymene harbor. Clay dust reaches her elbows; she has left a clear seat beside the kiln.

**Nature:** Warm but exact about borrowed things. She reads care in small acts, distrusts theatrical promises and does not make guests responsible for her grief.

**Priorities:** Keep the household and kiln supplied; Offer welcome without bondage; Recover tools by agreed terms.

**Busy with:** Trimming traveler_lamp while empty oars sound offshore.

**Wants:** Loan a useful lamp on clear terms and see a safe crossing restored.

**Will not:** She will not call a declined favor a debt, pledge a guest to a god or lend another person’s possessions.

**Private knowledge:**

- She made traveler_lamp: it is ordinary.
- She watched new toll lettering arrive before a replacement crossing pin.
- Her own brother’s boat returned empty; the oar sounds have never answered a question.

> Borrow it if you need it. Tell me when it comes back; the lamp cannot arrange that.

### Dion — Ferryman and sounding-pole keeper (dion)

**Where / visible:** Poseidon’s broken crossing. He checks the water before answering a demand for speed.

**Nature:** Cautious, dry and unwilling to confuse urgency with a safe load. He keeps promises literally and knows that fear can make an oath less than a choice.

**Priorities:** Return passengers safely; Keep the boat within its load; Attend a family burial freely.

**Busy with:** Sounding the channel and unloading the stopped cart.

**Wants:** Repair the crossing and find a freely agreed time for his family obligation.

**Will not:** He will not overload the ferry, require an oath or treat fear as consent.

**Private knowledge:**

- One failed pin is visible; the entire road is not swallowed.
- He holds funeral_coin for a family contribution, not a universal toll to the dead.
- He has seen brine enter after a knowingly false sworn load; unsworn safe passage is possible.

> The sea has heard your urgency. The boat still has a limit.

### Philon — Accountant of the granary temple (philon)

**Where / visible:** Demeter’s granary. Ink stains his thumb; he asks which document the accusation names.

**Nature:** Anxiously precise. He separates requests from grants, but fears exposing the compromises that once kept his position secure.

**Priorities:** Preserve traceable records; Prevent unauthorized allocations; Correct his own evasions without inventing excuses.

**Busy with:** Reconciling the unsigned feast demand against household shares.

**Wants:** A witnessed decision on allocation and permission to correct the misleading copy.

**Will not:** He will not forge a signature or certify a rumor as a receipt.

**Private knowledge:**

- The feast request is unsigned; Xanthe requested supplies without claiming authority to take them.
- Seed and household grain have separate marks.
- He copied a prior promise twice to postpone an argument; the copy made no second lawful grant.

> A request is not permission. I have columns for both, which is apparently provocative.

### Ione — Independent courier (ione)

**Where / visible:** Hermes' market of witnesses. Her traveling case stays within reach while she gives directions.

**Nature:** Alert and private. She resists being turned into a prop in a stranger’s heroic account and distinguishes custody from entitlement.

**Priorities:** Deliver only to an agreed recipient; Keep messages private; Refuse changed work on unacceptable terms.

**Busy with:** Checking the permitted delivery line.

**Wants:** Resolve delivery with a willing recipient and retain her own choices.

**Will not:** She will not surrender cargo to claimed destiny or put a private letter on the public oath tablet.

**Private knowledge:**

- In genuine/counterfeit she received a sealed cup; in legend only correspondence about the story.
- The palace-marked letter claims freely given years can be held; she has never witnessed that capability.
- Leon can identify the old office mark if she chooses to show it.

> You may ask who sent me. You may not turn that question into ownership.

### Thaleia — Keeper of seed and household grain (thaleia)

**Where / visible:** Demeter’s granary. A separate blue bowl holds viable seed.

**Nature:** Patient with need, hard on performative generosity that spends somebody else future. She notices who carries a sack and who only names a virtue.

**Priorities:** Preserve viable seed; Feed households through an explained allocation; Protect workers from compulsory vows.

**Busy with:** Testing grain and preparing portions under the rootlike shadows.

**Wants:** An allocation people can inspect and enough seed for another season.

**Will not:** She will not accept a sacred title as authority to empty the seed bay.

**Private knowledge:**

- Household grain is sound; requests conflict.
- Three distribution work lots remain after the initial stock count.
- Burning marked seed for display is the announced taboo; eating an ordinary meal is not.

> Eat the seed if you choose. You will have to explain spring to the children.

### Makar — Bronze smith and repairer (makar)

**Where / visible:** The statue and bronze works. His patched measuring string has deliberate knots.

**Nature:** Proud of work that holds, suspicious of praise that replaces payment. Injury is a practical danger, never evidence that a person deserves less dignity.

**Priorities:** Protect helpers; Complete sound repairs; Receive agreed payment and credit.

**Busy with:** Testing the lifting pawl before casting the crossing pin.

**Wants:** Safe access to the casting area and a clear account of repair stock.

**Will not:** He will not call an unsafe brace divine will or compel a helper to swear.

**Private knowledge:**

- The statue recess was opened long before these travelers arrived.
- The worn pawl can be safely demonstrated.
- The harbor four work lots include stock, fitting and transport; a forge vow is not required to buy work.

> The god may admire your courage. I would prefer you stood outside the chalk.

### Xanthe — Steward of the borrowed feast (xanthe)

**Where / visible:** Palace of the borrowed feast. She checks servants’ seating before the high table.

**Nature:** Generous and theatrical, defensive before guests. She loves pleasure but recognizes the cruelty of demanding that another person display it.

**Priorities:** Feed without humiliation; Keep exits open; Keep workers and guests safe.

**Busy with:** Moving benches away from the unsafe eastern aisle.

**Wants:** A smaller affordable feast with genuinely open seats and exits.

**Will not:** She will not demand intoxication, intimacy, an oath or life-years as a price of hospitality.

**Private knowledge:**

- The roof rope needs unloading before guests enter.
- bronze_mask is a stage object with a strange shadow, not a power of command.
- The grain request is unsigned. Accepting sacred hosting means knowingly barred exits carry the stated vine consequence.

> A feast ought to leave room for people. That includes the ones who say no.

### Leon — Exile, name-keeper and burial worker (leon)

**Where / visible:** The road of uncounted names. He lets a mourner finish before repeating a name.

**Nature:** Reserved and exact about names. He distrusts speeches that erase the dead person they claim to honor, and gives mourners time to choose silence.

**Priorities:** Open a safe burial route; Record only permitted names; Keep private grief out of bargaining.

**Busy with:** Clearing the gate and copying names with permission.

**Wants:** A usable path and family control over disclosure.

**Will not:** He will not conscript mourners, sell names as gossip or require a stranger funeral contribution.

**Private knowledge:**

- The old boundary stone places the public verge outside the palace claim.
- The described seal is a palace office mark, not a divine signature.
- The stones echo only names freely spoken there; they cannot reveal private identities or verdicts.

> Tell me the name they used, if you want it written. Silence is an answer too.

### Clotho — Spinner (clotho)

**Where / visible:** The loom, if encountered. Her attention follows a strand just appearing.

**Nature:** Curious, amused and occasionally giddy; concerned with beginnings rather than a bearer’s virtue.

**Priorities:** Attend beginnings; Remain within her jurisdiction.

**Busy with:** Attending the appearance of another thread.

**Wants:** Continue her work and address only what belongs to beginnings.

**Will not:** She will not promise an ending, choose a donor or reopen engagement after anomaly naming.

**Private knowledge:**

- Beginnings are hers; duration and a bearer’s choices are not.
- Rumor does not establish the existence of a relic.

> A beginning, yes. What you do after it is still unwritten.

### Lachesis — Measurer (lachesis)

**Where / visible:** The loom, if encountered. She works without looking for approval.

**Nature:** Precise, dry and impatient with repetition; measure gives her no moral jurisdiction.

**Priorities:** Account for measure; Reject invented entitlement.

**Busy with:** Measuring a different thread.

**Wants:** Continue accurate measurement within her role.

**Will not:** She will not decide who should donate or promise happiness.

**Private knowledge:**

- Allotment is hers; fulfillment and deservingness are not.
- A recipient’s assertion cannot establish an honest gift.

> An amount is a measure. It is not a promise that you will enjoy it.

### Atropos — Cutter (atropos)

**Where / visible:** The loom, if encountered. The shears are tools in use, not a theatrical threat.

**Nature:** Spare and unhurried; concerned with endings, not the worth of the life before them.

**Priorities:** Attend endings; Decline judgments outside her role.

**Busy with:** Finishing the thread already before her.

**Wants:** Continue the work of endings.

**Will not:** She will not punish refusal, declare virtue or borrow another Fate’s authority.

**Private knowledge:**

- The end is hers; the preceding story is not.
- An absent thread offers no object for her work.

> The ending is my concern. You have brought several other questions.
