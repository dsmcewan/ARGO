# Encounters — referee edition

Use these situations when reached or legitimately reported. They are not chapters to complete in order. Each offers practical stakes, peaceful approaches and an exit. No scene requires a relic, funeral, battle with a god or meeting the Fates. Named participants do not imply that a distant person witnessed anything.

State material danger, announced divine taboo and meaningful costs before commitment. Routine safe inspection needs no roll. A standard uncertain manual check uses d20 + relevant bonus >15; ties fail. Use [RULES.md](RULES.md) for damage and other procedures. No roll creates another person’s consent. Divine effects below are human-refereed campaign rules, not actions this ARGO adapter implements.

## A light, with ordinary terms (e_lamp)

**Place / people:** Kymene harbor; Melia.

**Frame:** Melia fits a replacement wick while oars knock from an empty boat. The grain and crossing notices are visible beside her.

**Approaches, not an exhaustive menu:**

- Borrow the lamp on an exact return checkpoint and damage agreement; supply your own oil.
- Ask directions, buy ordinary equipment if offered, or look for unrelated work.
- Listen to the oars without assuming they are a conversation or a command.

**Costs and risks to explain first:**

- No advance deposit. Oil costs 1 obol and burns for six exploration turns under RULES.md.
- Name a mutually accepted return checkpoint, defaulting to the next agreed harbor return; neither side silently changes it. Actual damage must be disclosed; any agreed repair is capped at ordinary 5-obol replacement.
- Discussing the terms takes one turn if the table advances time for it. A spoken promise alone does not transfer custody.

**Evidence:** Melia saw repair money represented by toll lettering while the pin remained broken. Her observation is a lead, not an obligation.

**If ignored:** Melia keeps the lamp unless a real transfer occurs. She may carry it herself or seek another willing carrier; no curse, hidden debt or missed ending follows refusal.

**Exit:** Take either harbor route; leave the lamp unless Melia actually hands it over. No funeral service or oath is a loan condition.

## The seed and the feast (e_grain)

**Place / people:** Demeter’s granary; Philon, Thaleia.

**Frame:** Root-shaped shadows reach beneath the sacks. Philon holds an unsigned request; Thaleia has posted the seed taboo beside the blue door.

**Approaches, not an exhaustive menu:**

- Inspect the list and grain with permission; a safe one-turn count reveals the unsigned demand without a roll.
- Carry an authorized copy to Xanthe and negotiate a smaller feast or willing contributions.
- Help an agreed distribution stage, or leave the keepers to their own decisions.

**Costs and risks to explain first:**

- An inspection costs one turn; carrying a copy uses printed route times.
- An actual distribution stage spends one finite grain-house work lot.
- Knowingly burning blue-marked seed for display, after the posted warning is understood, makes the burned handful become biting husks for 1d4 manual damage to the burner. Ordinary eating or refusing this scene is not that taboo.

**Evidence:** The demand and grant are different documents. The root-shadows are real, but do not prove the grain is spoiled or identify a guilty person.

**If ignored:** No instant famine is invented. At the next agreed project review, funded work proceeds only if its prerequisites hold; unresolved allocation can pause it.

**Exit:** Use any granary route without taking sacks or swearing an oath; refusal assigns no blame score.

## What the boat has been told (e_crossing)

**Place / people:** Poseidon’s broken crossing; Dion.

**Frame:** Dion stops a handcart by a loose rail. Wet footprints end at an oath stone whose words can be read before anyone approaches.

**Approaches, not an exhaustive menu:**

- Inspect safely, then arrange a repair stage or an unsworn safe ferry passage.
- Split a load or leave cargo behind by explicit agreement.
- Choose to swear an accurate manifest only if you want that obligation, knowing its cost.

**Costs and risks to explain first:**

- Inspection takes one turn. Agreed ordinary ferry passage costs 1 obol per person with carried kit; bulky loads need a separately accepted price.
- Knowingly false sworn cargo causes that boat to take brine; an immediate return or unloading at the near bank is possible. No one must take the oath.
- A forced loaded crossing risks falling and cargo loss; state the danger before a manual Dexterity check against 15. No automatic drowning follows a refusal.

**Evidence:** The broken pin and optional oath are distinct hazards. Repair evidence leads to Makar, not to a required sacrifice.

**If ignored:** Dion keeps heavy carts off the span. The repair project advances only through actual funded stages at an agreed review.

**Exit:** Both approaching routes reach the safe bank without crossing. Back away freely or return inland.

## The witness with a borrowed voice (e_courier)

**Place / people:** Hermes' market of witnesses; Ione.

**Frame:** Ione offers one delivery line for inspection while the bronze tablet repeats a completed public bargain.

**Approaches, not an exhaustive menu:**

- Read only the permitted line; identify the office mark with a willing source.
- Negotiate lawful inspection or a changed delivery in genuine/counterfeit; in legend investigate the letter without inventing a cup.
- Witness informally or decline; use of the supernatural tablet is optional.

**Costs and risks to explain first:**

- Reading the offered line takes one turn and grants no access to the rest.
- Any carriage price, recipient or return term must be accepted before handover.
- The tablet warns: knowingly sign a false claim and it publicly repeats that signer and statement until publicly corrected. It reveals no unrelated secrets or private history.

**Evidence:** The palace mark identifies an office. Neither it nor the tablet proves the letter’s claims about years.

**If ignored:** Ione retains custody and seeks another willing witness. No off-screen theft is generated merely to force pursuit.

**Exit:** Every ordinary market route stays open. Declining to witness creates no magical obligation.

## The host who cannot leave (e_feast)

**Place / people:** Palace of the borrowed feast; Xanthe.

**Frame:** The mask shadow smiles after it has been put down. Xanthe moves benches out of the marked eastern aisle and props the doors open.

**Approaches, not an exhaustive menu:**

- Move the meal outdoors or reduce its scale, preserving welcome without forced display.
- Negotiate an affordable share with the granary, or attend without intoxication or vows.
- Inspect the mural with permission; it shows past movement, not a current inventory.

**Costs and risks to explain first:**

- Moving agreed benches takes two turns. A plain meal is 1 obol; an offered free portion has no retroactive price.
- Knowingly loading the marked unsafe aisle risks timber failure; state danger before any uncertain manual check.
- An announced sacred-host obligation has a precise cost: knowingly bar a guest’s departure after accepting that role and vines root the host’s own chair until the exit is opened. Nobody is forced to accept hosting.

**Evidence:** The damaged mural depicts a vessel leaving the quarry. The shadow confers no power to make anyone agree.

**If ignored:** Xanthe keeps the unsafe aisle closed and can postpone or shrink the feast. Party absence does not force a collapse, sacrifice or supernatural offense.

**Exit:** Leave by any palace route or depart the feast early. No intoxication, intimacy, oath or gift is owed.

## The maker’s chalk line (e_bronze)

**Place / people:** The statue and bronze works; Makar.

**Frame:** Makar braces a lifting arm beside an empty statue recess. One hammerstroke answers from the cold stone.

**Approaches, not an exhaustive menu:**

- Wait for the brace, inspect the pawl safely and offer agreed assistance.
- Compare the old sketch with current removal marks instead of searching until a relic appears.
- Arrange an ordinary paid repair or carry a message without taking a forge vow.

**Costs and risks to explain first:**

- Safe inspection takes one turn; agreed assistance takes two turns before a project review.
- Harbor work stock is already budgeted; a private commission needs its own accepted price.
- Releasing a loaded worn pawl risks crushing. In addition, one who knowingly breaks an optional sworn safety statement by releasing it under another worker’s load suffers the announced searing handle for 1d4 manual damage. Do not demand a vow to obtain service.

**Evidence:** The removal marks predate this party. An old drawing can be outdated or invented according to the fixed variant.

**If ignored:** The arm remains braced if work is unsafe. The recess remains empty in every variant; the world does not reward persistence by moving a cup there.

**Exit:** Use either ordinary route. No blood offering, battle or proof of worth is necessary to depart.

## Names that will not stay (e_names)

**Place / people:** The road of uncounted names; Leon.

**Frame:** At dusk the stones repeat only names willingly spoken there. Leon has found a boundary stone beside the disputed gate.

**Approaches, not an exhaustive menu:**

- Inspect the boundary with permission and negotiate the safe public verge.
- Help clear a funded section, offer private company, or ask what the family wants recorded.
- Decline public naming or burial work without penalty.

**Costs and risks to explain first:**

- Inspecting the stone takes one turn; a staged clearance takes two turns and one authorized work lot.
- Dion’s funeral_coin remains his. Any contribution is separately chosen; no visitor owes a mandatory coin.
- The posted taboo concerns knowingly erasing another family’s memorial name for profit: the offender’s newly written public name washes away until repair is made or accepted. It erases neither memory nor identity.

**Evidence:** The boundary supports a public path. The echoes give no private identity, soul judgment or answer about immortality.

**If ignored:** Families decide their own next step. No undead attack appears because strangers refuse assistance. The finite project can proceed at agreed reviews.

**Exit:** Take either route away. Silence is valid, and no family preference is assumed for every mourner.

## Work already in progress (e_loom)

**Place / people:** The loom, if encountered; Clotho, Lachesis, Atropos.

**Frame:** If an encounter has independently been established, three women continue three distinct tasks. No throne, merchant stall or quest board awaits.

**Approaches, not an exhaustive menu:**

- Ask a question within one sister jurisdiction and accept bounded speech or silence.
- Listen, decline or leave. The encounter owes no solution.
- Discuss possible gifts only with the actual living donors, elsewhere and freely; no sister selects them.

**Costs and risks to explain first:**

- No entrance payment or route roll guarantees arrival.
- Time actually spent is a manual record, not an automatic age debit.
- Questions do not purchase private history, future choices or another sister’s authority.

**Evidence:** Jurisdiction limits authority; it is not a puzzle whose solution combines the sisters into omniscience.

**If ignored:** The Fates continue their work. Departure creates no unfinished quest, missed reward or punishment.

**Exit:** Leave immediately by the departure established for this particular encounter. No ordinary route promises a return.

## Rumors with sources — never a player handout

The text is a possible attributed utterance, not knowledge delivered by loading a card. Let the named person decide whether to speak in this scene. Attribute a quoted false claim so an NPC does not accidentally endorse it. Actual delivery records speaker, asserted source, acquisition cursor and legitimate witnesses; a cast list or faction membership proves none of these.

G / C / L mean genuine / counterfeit / legend. Labels evaluate the substantive claim in the starting world; a mixed quote can be partial. The claim about the sketch location in rumor_06 is what is outdated or false, even though a speaker can truly report that the sketch exists. Keep these labels, private basis and hidden variant out of player/narrator inputs and retrieval rankings. Reassess only after actual events without rewriting original provenance.

### Referee evidence

- **rumor_01:** Melia saw that delivery. It establishes an order of purchases, not every council member’s motive.
- **rumor_02:** The quoted demand is false; the pin and attribution are real. The optional sworn-manifest taboo is separate and never demands life.
- **rumor_03:** Thaleia knows the local posted taboo. It applies to knowingly burning marked planting grain for display, not ordinary meals or refusal of work.
- **rumor_04:** Grain is sound. He copied a promise to postpone conflict; competing requests are not two lawful grants. The real root-shadows do not prove the curse claim.
- **rumor_05:** The letter exists in all variants. Only genuine establishes its capability claim. Ione’s lack of personal demonstration is accurate in all.
- **rumor_06:** The quoted location claim is outdated after removal in genuine/counterfeit, fictional in legend. The present recess is empty in all three.
- **rumor_07:** The claim of compulsion is false; the wrong shadow is real. The host exit taboo grants no command over guests.
- **rumor_08:** Leon found the stone and can show it. Access still requires safe practical work, not a narrated teleport through stone.
- **rumor_09:** The past injury had a mechanical cause, not mockery punishment. A separately announced knowingly false safety vow can incur the distinct searing-handle consequence.
- **rumor_10:** Dion heard this from one family only. It establishes no preference for every mourner and no knowledge of an afterlife verdict.
- **rumor_11:** The Fates belong to this continuity; Ione has conflicting accounts of access. A successful rumor check cannot manufacture a route.
- **rumor_12:** The quoted youth promise is false even in genuine. No seeded seller, offer or rumor creates custody, a donor or an effective method.

Melia offers ordinary lamp terms while independent grain and crossing notices remain visible. Location: Kymene harbor.

- Agree an exact return checkpoint and actual custody.
- Ask directions or buy ordinary equipment.
- Decline and leave Melia free to carry it herself or seek another willing carrier.

**Declining:** No debt, curse or locked opportunity. The lamp remains hers unless explicitly transferred.

### A list that can be read (hook_grain)

The granary seeks a willing witness or carrier; its notice promises no relic reward. Location: Demeter’s granary.

- Read the permitted allocation documents.
- Negotiate paid work.
- Watch the public process or refuse.

**Declining:** The grain house retains its finite problem and project; the traveler is not assigned responsibility for every hungry person.

### Work that should have been done (hook_crossing)

Dion refuses heavy carts on damaged stone; the harbor needs a safe pin and honest repair accounts. Location: Poseidon’s broken crossing.

- Help on accepted terms.
- Arrange safe unsworn ferry passage.
- Travel inland or seek other work.

**Declining:** The safety stop remains. No forced fall, oath or personal punishment follows refusal.

### A promise without a witness (hook_letter)

Ione wants one allowed line read without surrendering her case. Location: Hermes' market of witnesses.

- Witness only the offered line.
- Ask who recognizes its office mark.
- Decline and explore elsewhere.

**Declining:** Ione keeps custody and seeks another witness. No hidden hand redirects the party to the cup or loom.
