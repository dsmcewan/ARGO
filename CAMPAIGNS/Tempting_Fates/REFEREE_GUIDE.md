# Referee guide — Tempting Fates

Dark tragedy needs choices whose costs can be understood. It does not need a secret script in which every refusal is punished or every road ends at the cup. Gods can be frightening without becoming a single moral score. The three Fates measure their separate work; they never inherit the role of judging virtue.

## Prepare the table

1. Agree boundaries for horror, drowning, hunger, grief, coercion and bodily harm. Veil or replace material that players do not want. No missing abuse fragments are reconstructed. Never surprise a player with a taboo merely to force a punishment scene.
2. Create mortals with [RULES.md](RULES.md) and [CHARACTER_SHEET.md](CHARACTER_SHEET.md). Share [PLAYER_GUIDE.md](PLAYER_GUIDE.md), not this guide, the full atlas or world.json.
3. Privately select genuine, counterfeit or legend explicitly before world creation. Record the choice with the pack identity. Never reroll it because a player searches, fails a check or reads a guide.
4. Record initial positions, custody and three finite budgets. Ask what each traveler wants now; mortality is not a mandatory answer. Start with the ordinary lamp and independent work notices in [QUICKSTART.md](QUICKSTART.md).
5. Establish who records manual tabletop state and what supported ARGO actions, if any, are actually connected. No pure calculator or content loader authorizes a world change.

## Play the immediate situation

Frame observable state and stakes; ask for intent; explain cost and danger; resolve uncertainty; record actual outcomes and witnesses; offer an exit. Let routine acts work when nothing opposes them. Give every player a chance before asking one person for a second risky act. Silence, disconnection or a short session never supplies an action or consent.

Use failed checks for the consequence stated before rolling: a lost turn, refused access, a revealed dispute or a physical risk. Do not change failure into a compulsory bargain with a god. Success achieves the opportunity earned; it cannot rewrite the cup variant, private memories or another person’s decision. An NPC can decline despite eloquence.

## Horror, hubris and particular gods

The island contains real supernatural signs: empty-oar sounds, root-shaped grain shadows, wet footprints, a speaking witness tablet, a mask with a wrong shadow, a hammerstroke inside cold stone and publicly acquired names repeated at dusk. They unsettle ordinary work without proving every rumor about them. Nobody can mine them for private history or definitive afterlife explanations.

An announced taboo has four parts: the particular patron, the exact voluntary undertaking or knowingly chosen act, the evidence establishing a breach and the bounded consequence. Read the location rule before a player approaches it. Answer questions about alternatives. Do not change a taboo after a choice, count ignorance as knowledge, treat refusing work as offense or let a failed persuasion roll fabricate an oath. A god may have demands that characters contest; the referee need not endorse them as universal ethics.

### Local taboos at a glance

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Demeter / granary | Knowingly burn blue-marked planting grain for display after warning | The burned handful becomes biting black husks; 1d4 manual damage to the burner. No spread to household stocks. | Eat ordinary food, discuss allocation or leave. |
| Poseidon / crossing | Voluntarily swear a load manifest, then knowingly conceal its load | That boat takes brine; return or unload at the near bank. No automatic drowning. | Arrange a safe unsworn passage or take land routes. |

These are original tabletop effects with no hidden global score, automated recurring damage or repeat-farming benefit. One deliberate incident receives one adjudicated consequence; damage is not rerolled each narration or turn. If an effect is left unresolved, record that state and conditions for ending it rather than adding escalating punishment. The black husks and seared handle can injure or kill under manual HP rules: disclose that risk in advance. NPCs are not disposable exemptions from the same announced rules.

## Time and shared expeditions

One exploration turn is 600 seconds, a round 6 seconds and a watch 14,400 seconds. Add printed route times and resolved activity time to the manual record. No wall-clock absence advances a project. A ten-minute session is not automatically ten world minutes. Existing v1 advance_time changes the world clock, not age or life balances.

At a session checkpoint record location, time, custody, unresolved choices and information deliberately shared. A new party uses a compatible checkpoint. If simultaneous ARGO proposals conflict, serialize and refresh or reject the stale proposal; this kit adds no queue or transparent fork. Reconcile incompatible manual side-session histories openly before resolving the disputed action. Never move an absent player, merge private solo knowledge into a party or accept gifts through a proxy.

## Three finite projects

Review factions normally once per explicitly advanced watch, or after an agreed stage of actual work. A review is an opportunity to check prerequisites, not automatic progress. Apply min(requested steps, resources, remaining segments). Each applied segment consumes one stated work lot. Exhausted or completed projects apply zero. Never request more stages than actually happened. A pure preview changes no canonical state or time.

A work lot is a precommitted finite bundle of labor and material access for that project, not a coin anyone can spend elsewhere. There is no automatic replenishment, completion cascade, infinite harvest or LLM background simulation. Additional resources need an explicit funded agreement and manual record. Workers may withdraw; dangerous or unauthorized work pauses. Consent and life-years are never project resources.

### The harbor council (harbor_council)

**Members:** Melia, Dion, Makar. Membership does not pool private knowledge.

**Goal:** Restore safe passage while old toll promises and memorial duties compete for labor.

**Budget / progress:** 4 work lots remaining; 0/4 segments in A crossing that holds.

**Stages / trigger:** At an agreed review with willing workers, stock and safe access, spend one repair work lot for each actual completed stage: guide lines; cast pin; fit pin; load inspection. Complete stages in order. No real-time absence advances work.

**Visible progress:** Guide lines, a new pin, fitting work, then a posted load-test result.

**Completion:** Normal carts may use the inspected crossing. Repair does not settle toll claims, bind an oath or restore the drowned.

**Interruption:** Unsafe water, withdrawn labor or denied stock pauses the next stage. Resources never regenerate.

**Approaches:**

- Negotiate a public labor and stock account.
- Help a stage for agreed payment or a voluntary favor.
- Contest tolls while supporting repair; no vote transfers private property.

### The house of the sheaf (grain_house)

**Members:** Thaleia, Philon, Xanthe. Membership does not pool private knowledge.

**Goal:** Feed living households, preserve seed and choose a feast that does not consume either obligation.

**Budget / progress:** 3 work lots remaining; 1/4 segments in An allocation people can inspect.

**Stages / trigger:** The initial count is complete. At an agreed review, with keeper permission and workers, spend one distribution work lot for each remaining actual stage: publish proposed shares; hear households and revise; issue authorized portions. Objection is evidence, not a penalty.

**Visible progress:** A tally, proposed shares, visible corrections, actual issue receipts.

**Completion:** Agreed portions are issued and the seed reserve remains unless its custodians separately authorize another lawful use. No divine favor is promised.

**Interruption:** Missing households, disputed authority or withdrawn permission pauses that stage. Food never appears when a clock fills.

**Approaches:**

- Inspect with permission and copy the list.
- Agree a smaller feast without shaming hunger.
- Work a paid distribution shift without a compelled vow.

### The unburied road fellowship (unburied)

**Members:** Leon, Ione. Membership does not pool private knowledge.

**Goal:** Reopen burial access without selling names, conscripting grief or abandoning the living.

**Budget / progress:** 4 work lots remaining; 0/4 segments in A road with room for mourners.

**Stages / trigger:** At an agreed review with family permission and safe access, spend one road work lot per actual stage: locate boundary; agree public-verge access; clear and brace gate; escort a willing procession. The fellowship is living, not undead.

**Visible progress:** Boundary cords, access notice, braced gate, offered passage schedule.

**Completion:** A usable route and access agreement exist. No soul is judged, no dead person returns and no unwilling family is named.

**Interruption:** Refusal to participate has no penalty. Unsafe excavation or unresolved access pauses the relevant stage.

**Approaches:**

- Inspect the boundary with a willing witness.
- Offer labor, a cart or a safe alternative route.
- Help privately without publishing a name or joining the faction.

Literal example: harbor starts 0/4 with four work lots. One actual stage leaves 1/4 with three. A request for ten further steps applies at most three, and only if those three stages actually occurred. Grain starts 1/4 with three remaining lots; the initial count cannot be spent again.

## Fixed relic worlds

The quarry recess is empty in every variant. Ione initially holds measure_cup only in genuine/counterfeit; legend contains no physical alleged artifact. The same story can circulate in all three. Selecting a variant never gives a player or NPC its truth. Keep the host manifest and private seed out of narrator context.

### Genuine

The cup initially held by Ione genuinely mediates measured time freely and knowingly given by another living thread-bearer in this original continuity. It creates no years. A vessel was removed from the statue; old art is partial history, not present location authority. Use requires exact informed donor/recipient/amount/unit/knowledge/terms and, for ARGO, an authenticated host-verified receipt. Possession, wine, persuasion or an oath cannot substitute for consent. Measure changes neither age nor virtue, joy or anomaly probability. Selecting this variant discloses nothing to actors.

### Counterfeit

Ione holds an inert ceremonial copy. A vessel once left the recess; sellers later attached a life-extension story to its likeness. A touching vow or high roll cannot give it powers. A sincere consent offer does not make a failed method effective: debit no life for a demonstration. The old v1 transfer endpoint does not itself enforce campaign artifact identity; the host must deny unsupported method authorization.

### Legend

No measure_cup item exists. Ione carries correspondence about a vessel story, not a hidden substitute. The statue drawing was imaginative; its empty recess and ceremonial mural prove no life-extension artifact. Investigation can yield history and help living people without a consolation relic, compulsory route or promised immortality. The host does not authorize cup-mediated transfers here.

## Honest gifts: procedure, not a persuasion challenge

Use only if an effective method exists and the participants independently want to discuss it. No initial NPC is a designated donor. No civic project needs life-years. A genuine cup creates neither a free resource nor a promised tragic ending.

1. Establish known capability through legitimate evidence. Tell each prospective donor what is known, uncertain and impossible to promise: no restored youth, virtue or guaranteed happiness. A counterfeit demonstration authorizes no debit.
2. Give every living donor a genuine alternative to giving. Threats, captivity, food dependency, coercive magic or a claimed hospitality debt cannot be renamed generosity. A charisma check may help explanation; it cannot establish free informed choice.
3. Record donor, recipient, exact positive amount and unit, method and current custody, known effects, uncertainty, expiry and terms. The synthetic year is 360 days; confirm both people understand the unit. Do not hide conversions or require all remaining years.
4. Allow reconsideration. At commitment recheck the same terms, living donor, sufficient balance, unexpired/unused/unrevoked permission and actual informed confirmation. The tabletop referee records explicit acceptance. ARGO additionally requires an authenticated host to verify the exact receipt; a model yes, proxy, rumor or die result is not proof. Missing verification denies the transaction.
5. Apply debit and credit together once and retain the record. A failed method or rejected submission changes neither balance. Retries reuse the same request identity. Do not debit a donor off-screen to create an emotional twist.
6. Preserve consequences while leaving interpretation with the people. No automatic gratitude, regret, virtue or absence is authored for them. Any later permitted return would be a new explicit transaction, not rewriting old consent.

**Current integration limit:** v1 consent binds a donor, recipient, amount and terms but has no campaign artifact-method authorization field. The seed adapter does not enforce the genuine/counterfeit/legend operation rule. A human referee or an authenticated host must deny an unsupported method before proposing a transfer; an unrestricted v1 transfer endpoint is not proof of that policy. A production method-bound service requires a separately reviewed authority extension, outside this kit.

## The Fates and the boundary nobody explains

Clotho concerns beginnings, Lachesis measure, Atropos endings. None becomes a moral judge, donor finder or quest merchant. Their ambiguity comes from jurisdiction, not a trick designed to force the player to seek another sister. No mortal gains end_thread from equipment, combat, religion or a convincing line. Other gods’ local taboo rules do not widen Fate powers.

There is no anomaly probability, sacrifice recipe, threshold or scheduled ending. Do not make disappearance the reward for sufficient gifts. If a separately authorized fixture establishes an absent thread, ontological exposition stops immediately; naming by Clotho closes Fate engagement. Another speaker, persona or past query cannot reopen it. Preserve ordinary consequences and gift records without an explanatory epilogue.

Current v1 cutoff is world/branch-wide. This kit does not implement subject-scoped encounters. Never trigger disappearance automatically in a populated campaign while claiming other narratives remain unaffected. Leave it out of ordinary campaign triggers; isolate authorized tests. The source’s author-facing metaphysical discussion is not a revelation to reward players with.

## People and the unreachable onlooker

Let Melia interpret a carefully returned lamp through her nature; let Ione judge an opened case differently. Each impression belongs to its observer. A traveler carries no omniscient reputation table. Shared parties do not inherit solo confidences. A later account has a new speaker, source and acquisition time. Host perception, not roster membership, determines actual witnessing.

The narrator is tired, dry and sparingly sarcastic, outside the world. One possible observation, supported by visible evidence: "The toll board has fresh paint. The crossing is waiting for the same consideration." Do not turn the voice into a judge condemning refusal or an agent who can grant consent. In-world speakers cannot summon or bargain with it. Narration uses only a fresh audience-authorized page, never these referee notes or all observer impressions.

The existing complete traveler record means accepted speech and eligible committed actions since profile activation, not rejected drafts or recovered prehistory. Refresh between proposals and suppress stale prose when authorization or cutoff changes. Busy, ended or unaddressed NPCs may remain silent without a model call. Missing abuse-fragment behavior remains unspecified.

## What this kit does and does not execute

A human referee can run character creation, pointcrawl travel, hazards, ordinary combat, divine consequences and projects using these books and the separate rules reference. Keep HP, travel, supplies and project effects in that manual session record. Do not claim it is a second authoritative mutable database synchronized with ARGO.

The JSON is a strict starting catalog. Pure calculators accept explicit dice and numbers; they never draw, wound actors, spend food, move people or write events. The seed adapter builds existing v1 Genesis and character profiles, not an authoritative geography, combat, spatial-perception or curse engine. Integrated demonstrations may commit only supported authenticated custody, speech, impressions, observations and exact verified transfers. Unsupported canonical proposals must be refused rather than narrated as engine success. Future causal mechanics need a reviewed versioned extension.
